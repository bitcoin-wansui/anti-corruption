# Case Study: 赵群（Zhao Qun）

## Why this case was researched

This case was **not** opened because public evidence showed misconduct by 赵群.

The research was inspired by the August 2026 public announcement that 宫亚南, a former Yingdong District official, was under disciplinary and supervisory investigation. The investigation workflow then deliberately performed a simple upstream extrapolation along the organizational chain and asked whether public records concerning 赵群 revealed independently verifiable integrity-risk signals.

That research selection mechanism is **not evidence**. In particular:

> 宫亚南被查 ≠ 赵群有问题。

No guilt-by-association inference should be drawn from their formal leadership relationship.

## Scope

The research uses lawful, publicly accessible sources and focuses on:

- career and authority timelines;
- formal institutional relationships;
- disciplinary and judicial records;
- provincial inspection and rectification materials;
- public procurement and bidding;
- Yingdong District SOEs and financing platforms;
- investment-promotion relationships and temporal migration patterns;
- public disclosures and inconsistencies;
- counter-evidence and ordinary explanations.

It does not claim access to non-public disciplinary files, personal asset declarations, bank records, private communications, internal personnel files, or undisclosed beneficial ownership information.

## Conclusion discipline

The report does **not** conclude that 赵群 is corrupt, involved in 宫亚南's case, or personally responsible for every governance problem discovered within Yingdong District.

The narrower research conclusion is:

> 截至研究日期，在本研究覆盖的公开资料范围内，未发现足以支持赵群本人存在违法违纪或不当利益输送的可验证证据。

“未发现” does not mean “已证明不存在”. Public-source research can miss unavailable, removed, unindexed, non-public or incorrectly disclosed information.

Likewise, district-level inspection findings, SOE disclosure issues, related-party contexts or subordinates being investigated cannot automatically be attributed personally to the Party Secretary without additional evidence establishing knowledge, intervention, benefit, direction or responsibility.
