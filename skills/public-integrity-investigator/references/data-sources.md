# China-oriented public integrity data sources

Prefer primary sources and archive the exact page/date when possible.

## 1. Identity, appointments, duties
- Central/provincial/municipal/county government websites (`*.gov.cn`).
- Organization department / "先锋网" appointment notices and cadre public notices.
- Local NPC and CPPCC websites.
- Official government gazettes, leadership pages, work-division pages, meeting reports.
- Official party/government news centers and local official media for historical snapshots.

Search tactics:
- `姓名 任免`
- `姓名 简历`
- `姓名 分工`
- `姓名 党委书记/局长/秘书长`
- quote exact name + previous organization.

## 2. Discipline and supervision
- 中央纪委国家监委 `ccdi.gov.cn`.
- Provincial discipline commission sites, e.g. 安徽纪检监察网 `ahjjjc.gov.cn`.
- Municipal/county discipline commission releases when indexed.

Capture the procedural stage exactly: investigation, party discipline/government sanction, transfer to procuratorate, indictment, judgment.

## 3. Government procurement
- 中国政府采购网 `ccgp.gov.cn`.
- Provincial/municipal procurement platforms and government portals.

Fields to capture:
- purchaser;
- project title/ID;
- winner/supplier;
- amount;
- procurement method;
- announcement date;
- agents/experts when lawfully public;
- amendments/cancellations.

## 4. Public resource transactions / tenders
- 全国公共资源交易平台 `ggzy.gov.cn` and provincial/local sub-platforms.

Capture:
- tenderer;
- bidders;
- winning bidder;
- bid amount;
- project manager;
- award date;
- abnormal termination/re-tender;
- contract changes where public.

## 5. Corporate ownership and control
- 国家企业信用信息公示系统 `gsxt.gov.cn`.
- Listed-company disclosures and exchange filings where relevant.
- Company official announcements may supplement but not replace registry evidence.

Capture both current and historical relations when public:
- legal representative;
- shareholders/controllers;
- directors/executives;
- address changes;
- establishment date;
- branches/subsidiaries;
- administrative penalties/abnormal operation.

Commercial corporate databases may be used only as leads; corroborate material claims with primary records where possible.

## 6. Construction and engineering
- 全国建筑市场监管公共服务平台（四库一平台） and local housing/construction platforms.
- Government procurement/public resource platforms.

Capture:
- project owner;
- contractor;
- project manager;
- permits;
- contract value;
- start/completion;
- qualifications;
- penalties;
- major changes.

## 7. Land and real-estate project trail
- 中国土地市场网 / natural-resources land transaction announcements.
- Local natural resources bureaus and public resource exchanges.

Capture:
- parcel ID/location;
- transferor;
- acquirer;
- price/area;
- use;
- auction/listing date;
- related project/company.

## 8. Judicial and prosecutorial records
- 中国裁判文书网 when accessible.
- 人民法院公告/法院官网.
- 最高检 and local procuratorate releases.
- 中国执行信息公开网 for enforcement information where lawfully public.

Search not only the official's name but also key contractors/companies and known co-defendants.

## 9. Audit, budgets, debt, SOEs
- 审计署 and local audit bureaus.
- Government budget/final-account disclosures.
- State-owned assets regulator pages.
- Local SOE annual reports, bond prospectuses, rating reports, exchange disclosures.
- Financing platform bond documents can reveal projects, guarantees, related entities and government relationships.

## 10. Credit and administrative enforcement
- 信用中国 and local credit sites.
- Market-regulation, ecology, construction, transport and other regulator penalty pages.

## 11. Meetings,招商 and repeated co-occurrence
- Official government news;
-招商局/商务局 releases;
- company official news;
- local mainstream media.

Repeated meetings are weak evidence by themselves. Use them to identify counterparties to check against contracts, land, investment and ownership data.

## Source hygiene
- Prefer the original page over reposts.
- Record both publication date and underlying event date.
- When a current leadership page conflicts with historical evidence, treat it as a current snapshot, not proof of past office.
- Scraped/archived unofficial mirrors may help recover old pages but should be marked lower confidence.
