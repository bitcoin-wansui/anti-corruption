# Investigation report template

## Scope and caution
State the subject, date of research, public-only scope, and that anomalies are not findings of misconduct.

## 1. Identity and career timeline
Table: date | position | organization | evidence.

## 2. Authority map
Summarize what the subject could plausibly influence in each relevant period.

## 3. Key public network
List high-value nodes and typed relations. Prefer a compact relation table:
subject/entity A | relation | entity B | period | confidence | evidence.

## 4. Confirmed disciplinary/judicial facts
Only authoritative procedural/final findings. Preserve exact procedural language.

## 5. Anomaly signals worth further checking
For each signal:
- **Signal:** short name
- **Severity / evidence confidence**
- **Observed facts**
- **Why it matters**
- **Normal explanations / counter-evidence**
- **Next public records to inspect**

## 6. What the public evidence does NOT show
Explicitly list tempting but unsupported conclusions.

## 7. Next-step search plan
Prioritized specific searches/data sources.

## 8. Evidence ledger
Numbered sources with publisher, date, URL/citation and the precise fact each establishes.

# Required language discipline
Use:
- "公开资料显示"
- "存在时间上的相关性"
- "值得进一步核查"
- "尚无公开证据证明"
- "不能据此推断"

Avoid unless officially established:
- "腐败"
- "利益输送"
- "保护伞"
- "裙带关系"
- "他的马仔/他的人"
